# Third-party software and source material

The MIT license at the repository root covers original Lenz project code. It does
not replace licenses, copyrights or terms belonging to dependencies, copied source,
generated upstream code or downloaded research material.

- NEAR/Rust and JavaScript dependencies are pinned in their Cargo and npm lockfiles.
  Each dependency retains its upstream license.
- The Aurora local test tools use [EthereumJS](https://github.com/ethereumjs/ethereumjs-monorepo).
  Its packages retain their MPL-2.0 or MIT licenses as recorded in the npm lockfile.
  Installed package source is excluded from this repository.
- The checked-in NEAR vendor bundle retains the
  notices in [web/public/near-vendor/NOTICE.txt](web/public/near-vendor/NOTICE.txt)
  and their source headers. Current connector scope is documented in
  [web/near-connector/README.md](web/near-connector/README.md).
- The native Zcash executable and dependency caches are excluded. Source revisions
  are recorded in [zcash/source-lock.json](zcash/source-lock.json). The local signer
  patch modifies `zcash-devtool`, whose upstream package declares MIT OR Apache-2.0;
  consult that upstream project for its license texts.
- The local proof build uses Circom, circomlib, circomlibjs, snarkjs and related
  libraries. Generated browser dependency notices are copied by `web/build.mjs`.
  Those build products are not relicensed under the root license.
- The QR decoder and encoder retain their license texts in
  [web/public/qr-vendor/](web/public/qr-vendor/).
- [aurora/contracts/PaymentStepVerifier.sol](aurora/contracts/PaymentStepVerifier.sol)
  is generated by snarkjs and carries its GPL-3.0 notice and upstream copyright.
  It is not covered by the root MIT license.
- Technical documentation links to external references; citations do not establish original authorship of those sources.

Dependency or source attribution does not make its maintainers authors of Lenz's
Git commits, imply endorsement or extend an upstream audit to this application.
