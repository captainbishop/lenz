# Code of conduct

Everyone participating in Lenz discussions, issues and reviews should be able to
work without harassment or intimidation.

Be respectful and specific. Critique the work, explain disagreements and allow
people to correct mistakes. Do not use threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual
harassment, personal attacks or unwanted persistent contact. Do not disclose
someone's private information, credentials or conversations without consent.

The maintainer, [captainbishop](https://github.com/captainbishop), may remove content,
close discussions or restrict participation when necessary. Responses should consider
the behavior, its impact and repeated violations. This applies in project spaces and
when representing the project elsewhere.

Report conduct concerns privately through a contact channel explicitly published on
the maintainer's GitHub profile. If no private channel is available, ask the
maintainer to provide one without posting the incident's sensitive details. Reports
will be handled with care; no fixed response time is promised.
