About these figures

Loading history shares this wallet address with the trading network. History is saved encrypted on this device so you can view it offline. This does not move money or approve trades.

Figures cover available USDC perpetual trades from the last 30 days. P&L includes trading fees and excludes funding payments and open-position gains. Win and loss rates count closed portions of positions before fees, including break-even closes in the total. Other markets and wallets are separate.
