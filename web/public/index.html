WHEN YOU'RE OFFLINE

You can still
make an agreement.

01Make the agreement.

Open your wallet or a conversation. Choose the person and amount. Each device signs the payment or trade terms.

02Carry it between devices.

Save the encrypted file and share it through a local connection. The recipient opens it and checks the signature without contacting Lenz's server.

03Use what you receive.

A received payment can fund another payment. Its earlier signed agreements travel with it, so the next person can check that history.

Offline payments remain pending until online settlement. Saved swaps run only after approval.

THE NEXT LAYER

Programmable
agreements.

Lenz takes inspiration from Ethereum's programmability: money can follow rules that people choose, including rules for an escrow or a shared fund.

Offline payments and signed Rhea swaps are implemented on NEAR testnet. General contract execution is still ahead. The aim is to carry contract state and verifiable actions between devices.

BEFORE YOU START

A few useful answers.

Do I need to chat before I pay?

No. Open Wallet to send and receive directly. Payments are also available inside a conversation.

Can I use a private name?

Yes. Continue privately creates a name and symbol. No phone number or real name is required. Existing contacts can still recognise your account after a profile edit.

What happens if I clear browser data?

Clearing browsing history alone normally keeps site data. Clearing Lenz's site data removes this prototype's keys and local history. Recovery and moving accounts between devices still need to be built.

Where do Ethereum and NEAR fit?

Ethereum's programmable contract model is the inspiration. NEAR testnet handles payment settlement and Rhea swaps. Aurora currently has local verifier experiments; general contract execution remains in development.
